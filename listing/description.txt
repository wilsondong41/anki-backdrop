**Play a soft, blurred video or live stream behind your Anki cards while you study** — instead of covering your card text with a picture-in-picture window.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/CY0QPhw.png" width="640" alt="Frosted-glass card floating over a blurred backdrop">

## What it does

- **Frosted-glass card over video.** Your card sits on a centered, translucent frosted-glass plate so text stays easy to read while the video glows softly behind and around it. Works with stock Anki as well as theme and UI add-ons like Onigiri, Synapse, AMBOSS, and AnKing card templates.
- **Quick controls while studying.** A small tab on the right edge opens a compact panel that never steals keyboard focus from Space or 1–4:
  - Play / Pause (`Shift+P`), Hide picture (keep sound), Turn on / off
  - **Position** scrub bar for regular videos (automatically hidden for live streams)
  - **Backdrop** strength slider (from soft blur to clear) and **Volume** slider
  - **Stream quality** (`Auto`, `360`, `480`, `720`, `1080`) for live streams
  - Paste a link or one-click play your copied link or recent videos
- **Hold P to peek.** Hold **P** any time during review or on the deck overview to temporarily see the full video clearly; release to return to your card.
- **Auto-ducks for audio.** Automatically turns the backdrop down to 1/4 volume while Anki card audio, `{{tts}}`, or the Read Aloud add-on is speaking, then brings it back up.
- **Live streams & webcams.** Works with YouTube, YouTube Live, Twitch, direct `.webm`/`.mp4` links, and other `yt-dlp`-supported streams (no live chat, so it stays distraction-free).
- **Nothing saved to disk.** Regular videos stream directly in the player; live streams are converted on the fly in memory and stop immediately when paused or when Anki closes.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/9hLfxl1.png" width="640" alt="Right-edge quick controls panel with position, strength, volume and stream quality">

## Getting started

1. Open **Tools → Backdrop Video…** (or press **Ctrl+Shift+B** / **Cmd+Shift+B**).
2. Paste a YouTube, Twitch, or video link and click **Play**.
3. Use the small film-strip tab on the right edge while studying to adjust blur, volume, position, or switch recent videos.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/NYsV5RX.png" width="520" alt="Tools → Backdrop Video… dialog">

## Good to know

- **Live streams (`yt-dlp`):** Anki's built-in browser can't play H.264 live streams directly. The first time you play a live stream, Backdrop Video offers a one-click download of the free `yt-dlp` helper (~3 MB, kept in `user_files` across updates) and uses Anki's bundled `mpv` (Mac & Windows; Linux uses system `mpv`) to stream WebM in memory. Regular YouTube videos need no extra download.
- **Software rendering:** If Anki is set to software video rendering, blur is turned off automatically to keep CPU usage low.
- **Requires internet:** Videos stream live over your connection and aren't downloaded offline.
