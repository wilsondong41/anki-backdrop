**Play a soft, blurred video or live stream behind your Anki cards while you study** — instead of covering your card text with a picture-in-picture window.

## What it does

- **Frosted-glass card over video.** Your card sits on a centered, translucent frosted-glass plate with its own **Study card** translucency slider—plus a **True transparency** toggle if you also want the frosted blur and border to fade away as you slide down. Works with stock Anki as well as theme and UI add-ons like Onigiri, Synapse, AMBOSS, and AnKing card templates.
- **Multi-view (1–4 videos or streams at once).** Stack up to 4 videos or live streams behind your cards (2 split evenly, 3 arranged 2-on-top + 1-on-bottom, 4 in a 2×2 grid) without cropping landscape, vertical, or resized windows. Mute, save, or remove individual streams anytime, and give each one **its own volume slider** (the main Volume still sets them all).
- **Saved folders & multi-select.** Organize favorite videos or streams into compact folder pills right in the sidebar (`★` on any playing, recent, or browsed page). Use **Select** to pick up to 4 saved items across folders and launch them together in multi-view. Click `✎` to **rename** any saved link.
- **Quick controls while studying.** A small tab on the right edge opens a compact panel that auto-minimizes when you click back to study and never steals keyboard focus from Space or 1–4:
  - Play / Pause (paused videos freeze on their frame instead of going blank), Hide picture (keep sound), **Break zone**, Turn on / off
  - **Pause one video:** with 2–4 videos playing, each one has its own pause button, so one can freeze on its frame while the others keep going
  - **Per-video timestamp & `-10s` / `+10s` skip:** Click any non-live video's title in **Now Playing** (even with 2–4 videos playing at once) to open `-10s` / `+10s` skip buttons and a scrub slider for just that video, then click `↩` to return to `★` / Mute / `×`. Works for YouTube **and other sites' videos** too, each video picks up where you left it, and the picture holds its last frame while it jumps (no blank flash)
  - **Albums & playlists:** A link with several videos (YouTube playlists, or albums and multi-video posts on other sites) shows `‹ Video 3 of 14 ›` under the slider so you can pick a video. When one ends, the next one plays, and it remembers which video you were on
  - **Backdrop** blur/opacity, **Study card** translucency (+ **True transparency**), and **Volume** sliders
  - **Stream quality** (`Auto`, `360`, `480`, `720`, `1080`, `1440`, `4K`) and **Frame rate** (`Auto`, `15`, `24`, `30`, `45`, `Source`; hover any option for what it's good for) with a live FPS readout. Lower frame rates save CPU and battery, and the blur hides the difference
  - Paste any link, one-click play your copied link, manage **Saved** folders and **Recent** links (with `+` to play alongside, or `⇄` to **swap one stream out** when 4 are playing), or open the built-in tabbed **Search / Browse** pop-up (`🔍`) with multiple tabs, `★ Save…`, safe `+ Add` defaults when a stream is already playing, **Replace** that asks which stream to swap out, and **your own quick links** (`+` adds the page you're on; right-click to rename or remove)
- **Break zone.** Take a breather without inflating your Anki card timing or breaking Pomodoro timers (compatible with Pomodoro Bar and other Pomodoro add-ons). Your card fades out and the videos keep playing with no reload or blank screen. Count up, or tap a **5–30 minute timer** (it counts down from when you tap it, shows a draining bar, and the next break remembers it). With 2+ streams, **click one to fill the screen** (the others pause and go quiet), click again to go back, and **drag one onto another to swap places**. Press **Esc** or **Back to Study** to return; Space and 1–4 can't answer the hidden card meanwhile.
- **Live now dots.** Saved and recent streams (Twitch, Kick, YouTube Live, cam and chat rooms, and other `yt-dlp` sites) show a green dot when they're live and a red one when they're offline, and folder buttons show how many are live. Checked lightly, only while the panel is open, and remembered for a few minutes.
- **Hold P to peek.** Hold **P** any time during review or on the deck overview to temporarily see the full video clearly; release to return to your card. Works on every site and screen (Onigiri layouts too), even when a website grabs the keyboard, and stays clear if a new card appears mid-peek.
- **Auto-ducks for audio.** Automatically turns the backdrop down to 1/4 volume while Anki card audio, `{{tts}}`, or the Read Aloud add-on is speaking, then brings it back up.
- **Live streams & webcams.** Works with YouTube, YouTube Live, Twitch, Kick, direct `.webm`/`.mp4`/`.m3u8`/`.mpd` links, and other `yt-dlp`-supported streams (no live chat, so it stays distraction-free). Frame rate is automatic (`30–60 fps` depending on your computer and stream count) unless you pick one.
- **Plays videos other apps can't.** Many YouTube videos (most music, sports and other monetized uploads) and sites like Dailymotion, Reddit or GitHub refuse to play inside other apps. Backdrop notices, plays the video through the `yt-dlp` helper instead, remembers which YouTube links need it so they start faster next time, and tells you plainly if a page has no video to play.
- **Smooth screen changes.** Going between the deck overview and your cards keeps the last picture on screen while the video reconnects, so the backdrop never flashes black.
- **Nothing saved to disk.** Regular videos stream directly in the player; live streams are converted on the fly in memory and stop immediately when paused or when Anki closes.
- **Stay signed in (optional, off by default).** Tick **Stay signed in** in the Browse window to keep your website logins after Anki closes. Only each site's login cookie is kept (never your passwords), on this computer only, in Anki's folder (not synced, never uploaded). Untick it to be asked whether to sign out of everything and delete them.

## Getting started

1. Open **Tools → Backdrop Video…** (or press **Ctrl+Shift+B** / **Cmd+Shift+B**) to browse/pre-find videos, organize your **Saved Folders**, adjust display settings, or install/update the optional `yt-dlp` live-stream helper.
2. Or click the small film-strip tab on the right edge while studying to paste a link, browse, save into folders, adjust blur/transparency/volume, or add streams alongside what's playing.

## Good to know

- **Live streams (`yt-dlp`):** Anki's built-in browser can't play H.264 live streams directly. When a link needs the free `yt-dlp` helper (~3 MB, kept in `user_files` across updates), Backdrop Video asks once, says why, and **Download and Play** gets it and starts the video in one click (there's a **Don't show this again** option, and you can download/update it anytime in **Tools → Backdrop Video…**). It uses Anki's bundled `mpv` (Mac & Windows; Linux uses system `mpv`) to stream WebM in memory. Many YouTube videos play without it.
- **Software rendering:** If Anki is set to software video rendering, blur is turned off automatically to keep CPU usage low.
- **Requires internet:** Videos stream live over your connection and aren't downloaded offline.
