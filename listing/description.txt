**Play a soft, blurred video or live stream behind your Anki cards while you study** — instead of covering your card text with a picture-in-picture window.

## What it does

- **Frosted-glass card over video.** Your card sits on a centered, translucent frosted-glass plate with its own **Study card** translucency slider—plus a **True transparency** toggle if you also want the frosted blur and border to fade away as you slide down. Works with stock Anki as well as theme and UI add-ons like Onigiri, Synapse, AMBOSS, and AnKing card templates.
- **Multi-view (1–4 videos or streams at once).** Stack up to 4 videos or live streams behind your cards (2 split evenly, 3 arranged 2-on-top + 1-on-bottom, 4 in a 2×2 grid) without cropping landscape, vertical, or resized windows. Mute, save, or remove individual streams anytime.
- **Saved folders & multi-select.** Organize favorite videos or streams into compact folder pills right in the sidebar (`★` on any playing, recent, or browsed page). Use **Select** to pick up to 4 saved items across folders and launch them together in multi-view.
- **Quick controls while studying.** A small tab on the right edge opens a compact panel that auto-minimizes when you click back to study and never steals keyboard focus from Space or 1–4:
  - Play / Pause, Hide picture (keep sound), **Break zone**, Turn on / off
  - **Per-video timestamp & `-10s` / `+10s` skip:** Click any non-live video's title in **Now Playing** (even with 2–4 videos playing at once) to open persistent `-10s` / `+10s` skip buttons and a scrub slider for that video, then click `↩` anytime to return to `★` / Mute / `×`
  - **Backdrop** blur/opacity, **Study card** translucency (+ **True transparency**), and **Volume** sliders
  - **Stream quality** (`Auto`, `360`, `480`, `720`, `1080`, `1440`, `4K`) with live FPS readout
  - Paste any link, one-click play your copied link, manage **Saved** folders and **Recent** links (with `+` to play alongside), or open the built-in tabbed **Search / Browse** pop-up (`🔍`) with multiple tabs, `★ Save…`, and safe `+ Add` defaults when a stream is already playing
- **Break zone.** Take a breather cleanly without inflating your Anki card timing or breaking Pomodoro timers (compatible with Pomodoro Bar and other Pomodoro add-ons) while watching your video/stream clearly with a live break/rest countdown.
- **Hold P to peek.** Hold **P** any time during review or on the deck overview to temporarily see the full video clearly; release to return to your card.
- **Auto-ducks for audio.** Automatically turns the backdrop down to 1/4 volume while Anki card audio, `{{tts}}`, or the Read Aloud add-on is speaking, then brings it back up.
- **Live streams & webcams.** Works with YouTube, YouTube Live, Twitch, Kick, direct `.webm`/`.mp4`/`.m3u8`/`.mpd` links, and other `yt-dlp`-supported streams (no live chat, so it stays distraction-free). Adaptive `30–60 fps` (or set `multi_fps` in add-on config) keeps multi-stream CPU usage low.
- **Nothing saved to disk.** Regular videos stream directly in the player; live streams are converted on the fly in memory and stop immediately when paused or when Anki closes.

## Getting started

1. Open **Tools → Backdrop Video…** (or press **Ctrl+Shift+B** / **Cmd+Shift+B**) to browse/pre-find videos, organize your **Saved Folders**, adjust display settings, or install/update the optional `yt-dlp` live-stream helper.
2. Or click the small film-strip tab on the right edge while studying to paste a link, browse, save into folders, adjust blur/transparency/volume, or add streams alongside what's playing.

## Good to know

- **Live streams (`yt-dlp`):** Anki's built-in browser can't play H.264 live streams directly. When a live or incompatible stream needs the free `yt-dlp` helper (~3 MB, kept in `user_files` across updates), Backdrop Video prompts you (with a **Don't show this again** option) and also lets you download/update it anytime in **Tools → Backdrop Video…**. It uses Anki's bundled `mpv` (Mac & Windows; Linux uses system `mpv`) to stream WebM in memory; regular YouTube videos need no extra download.
- **Software rendering:** If Anki is set to software video rendering, blur is turned off automatically to keep CPU usage low.
- **Requires internet:** Videos stream live over your connection and aren't downloaded offline.
